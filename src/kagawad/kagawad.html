Conciliation Guidelines

Pursuant to the Local Government Code of 1991, Section 399-422, all disputes within Barangay jurisdiction must undergo mandatory conciliation proceedings prior to any court or police referral.

3-Stage Mediation Process:
  1. 1st Summons: Informal Mediation
  2. 2nd Summons: Lupon Conciliation Panel
  3. 3rd Hearing: Final Agreement or Certificate to File Action
