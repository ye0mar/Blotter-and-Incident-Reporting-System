How It Works

Simple steps to report and track incidents in Barangay Sta. Ana

1. Submit Report

Fill out the official blotter report form with incident details, respondent info, and date/location.

2. Official Review

Barangay officials and assigned Kagawad review the report and schedule mediation or investigation.

3. Resolution

Track updates and hearing dates in your schedule until an amicable settlement or official resolution is reached.
